## Notes

- The "System Fonts" include those
across different operating systems. They
might not all be available. The source
list came from [Modern Font Stacks](https://modernfontstacks.com/)
which I then ran through my Mac (macOS 15.x) and Windows 10
machines to pull the aspect values. 
Fonts outside of those aren't captured
here. [Pull requests are welcome for those](https://github.com/alanwsmith/fonts.alanwsmith.com).
